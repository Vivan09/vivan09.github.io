VoMu Trend Hunter

VoMu Trend Hunter is the studio's internal research tool. It helps us decide what game to make next by checking whether people actually search for a trend.

  • It watches public trend sources: Google Trends, YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, Twitch and Roblox.
  • It checks Google Play search suggestions and the games that already exist for a name.
  • With the account owner's permission, it reads keyword search-volume estimates (Keyword Planner) from the studio's own Google Ads account through the Google Ads API.

The tool only reads keyword planning data. It does not create, change or pause campaigns or ads, and it does not touch billing. It is used only by the studio owner and is not offered to other people.

How the tool handles Google user data is described in the Privacy Policy.
